About us who we are


Who we are

DAISA is a project engineering firm that was established in 1998 by Osvaldo Barrera and Sigifredo Peña, with the purpouse of fulfilling the metal-mechanic industry needs in Monterrey city.

DAISA has more than 15 years of experience in the project engineering management and development, working for companies such as HYLSA (Ternium), Galvak, IMSA, Fuchs, etc.

With a rich and proud beginning, DAISA is built by strong foundations in the branches of metal mechanic, civil, electric and automatization engineering services covering small to large scale projects in industries such as the glass, food, cement, quemical and energetics.

Mission and Vision
