Services what we do

A few of the steps our workteam follows in a project are:

  1. Reception of the basic engineering from the customer. Documentation, analysis and verification.
  2. Visiting of the work area, data gathering (Process and drawings), topographical surveying. Validation through customer meetings.  
  3. Deliver of the planning propousal to the customer with completion deathlines for client apporval. 
  4. Weekly fordward of the Engineering progress report.
  5. Realisation of a quality  and optimized engineering fulfilling the nexts essentials: 
    6. 
	
    • Calculation reports, desing and selection of Drawing components.
    • Especificaction document and/or Design data sheet creation
    • Accesories and equipment selection in accordance of the Design data and/or the especificactions documents.
    • Market's spear point accesories and equipment selection of easy adquisition.
    • AutoCad, MicroStation, etc. drawings
    • Drawing creation under client's standard such as: Layers, Line types, Size, Frame, etc.
    
	
  6. Drawing progress review  according to client's especifications.
  7. Only two drawing prints tops, before the final delivery
  8. Engineering following during installation only if its deployed in the next two months after final delivery. 
  9. Drawings update (As built) after end of the project construction works, separated costs.
  10. Delivery of final drawings updated in digital format, same as especifications, calculation reports in the Final project workbook.
